How to use the hub

This is the shared workspace for the three of us. One link, four pages: the vision, our files, where we are, and a log of every meeting. Here is what each one is for and how to use it. Nothing to install, nothing to sign in to.

The four pages

Visionthe company in one page

What Beam does, who we are, and the story behind the name. This is the page to open, or send, when someone asks what the company is.

Repositoryour shared files

Our decks, resumes, the business case, workshop notes, anything the three of us should have in one place. Drag a file onto the box, or click to choose one (up to 50 MB). Click a file’s name to open it, Download to save it, or the × to remove it. Everyone sees the same list.

Progresswhere we are

The top shows the next deliverable and who owns it, then what comes after. Change a status from the dropdown and the other two see it. Below is a message board: put your name in once, then post updates, blockers, dates, and wins as they happen.

Meetingsthe retrospective log

A running timeline of every meeting. Paste the notes from your AI notetaker and Claude files the meeting the same way every time: a short summary, the decisions made, and each action item tagged with who owns it. No AI notes? Add one by hand. See the steps below.

Logging a meeting

1
Grab the notes
After a call, open your AI notetaker (Otter, Fathom, Granola, Zoom, Claude, whatever you use) and copy the whole summary or transcript.
2
Open Meetings and paste
Go to the Meetings page. Set the date, put your name in, and paste the notes into the box under Add from AI notes.
3
Parse and add
Click Parse & add to the timeline. Claude reads the notes and files a clean record: what was discussed, the decisions, and the action items with owners (Nate, Pat, Mike, All, or Unassigned).
4
It saves itself
The meeting lands on the timeline and the three of us see the same thing. If something reads wrong, hit remove on it and paste the notes again.
–
No AI notes that time?
Use Or add a meeting by hand on the same page to type what you discussed and the progress made. One of us just needs to put it in.

Good to know

Open by the link
There is no password. Anyone with the link can open the hub, so keep the link among the three of us.
Put your name in once
The Progress board and Meetings remember your name on your own device, so your posts and entries are signed as you.
Everything is shared and live
Files, statuses, posts, and meetings are the same for all three of us and save as you go. Open a page again to pull the latest.
Change anything by asking
Open the brotherbeam-hub project in your own Claude at claude.ai/code and ask for any change to any page. You do not need to know how it is built.
