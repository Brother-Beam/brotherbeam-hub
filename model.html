The name

Why Brother Beam

Sistering

When a beam must carry more than it was built for, a builder does not tear it out. He bolts a second beam alongside it. In the trade that move is called sistering, and the new beam is the sister. The old one keeps carrying; the sister shares the load. That is what we do to a business: keep what works, and build the new structure, AI included, beside it.

The name

Sistering is the method. We made it Brother Beam because of who built it. Pat and Mike are brothers, and Nate was family long before this was a company. Through the hard years around 2008 the families leaned on each other and stayed standing. Coming alongside to carry the load is just how we grew up.
