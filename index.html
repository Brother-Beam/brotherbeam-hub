A consultation and advisory agency for the changes that decide a business’s next decade: handing it on, bringing in new technology, or growing without breaking what works. Guided by founders who have done it, not just advised on it.

What we help with

Three kinds of transformation

Succession
Handing the business on, or stepping back, without losing what makes it work. The plan, the people, and the systems that let it run without you.
Technology
Bringing AI and new systems in beside what already works, not ripping it out. What to build, what to buy, what to leave alone, and what it costs to run.
Scaling
Growing without the business breaking: an operating model, a cadence, and measurement that keep up as the company gets bigger.

The name

Why Beam

When a beam must carry more than it was built for, a builder does not tear it out. He bolts a second beam alongside it. In the trade that move is called sistering: the old beam keeps carrying, the sister shares the load. That is what we do for a business — keep what works, and build the new structure beside it. We called it Beam, and it was built by two brothers and a friend who was family long before this was a company.
