Plug in and build

No code. You talk, your Claude builds.
1 · Connect your Claude
  1. Accept the GitHub invite. Go to github.com/Brother-Beam, sign in, and click Join. It is also waiting in your email.
  2. Open the hub in Claude. Go to claude.ai/code, connect GitHub when it asks, and open the brotherbeam-hub project.
  3. Say hello. It already knows the whole company. Ask it anything about Brother Beam to get going.
2 · Build with it
  1. Say what you want. In plain words: “Make a page for our pricing,” or “Add our three target industries to the model page.”
  2. See it first. Your Claude builds it and shows you before anything goes out.
  3. Publish. Tell it to publish and the page is live on the hub for all of us in about a minute.

That is how this whole hub was built. You never touch code.
